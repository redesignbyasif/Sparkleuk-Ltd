Keep Your Property Safe, Legal & Insured with In‑House PAT Testing.

Most cleaning companies stop at surface presentation — leaving landlords, letting agents, HMO operators and office managers to chase a separate contractor just to get portable appliances tested before tenants move in or policies renew.

Sparkleuk Ltd handles both in‑house. Whether booked alongside an end‑of‑tenancy deep clean, a holiday let turnaround, an office contract, or as a standalone electrical safety visit, our qualified PAT testing service inspects your appliances and provides clear compliance documentation without contractor delays.
